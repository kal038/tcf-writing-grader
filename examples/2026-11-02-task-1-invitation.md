# Inviter un ami au restaurant

## Reusable Vocabulary and Collocations
- **Ça te dirait de… ?** — Would you like to…?
- **réserver une table** — to book a table

## Reusable Grammar
Use the conditional to make a polite suggestion: « On pourrait dîner ensemble. »

No notable mistakes identified.

## Active Recall and Transfer
Invite a colleague to lunch. Include a time, a location, and a polite suggestion.
